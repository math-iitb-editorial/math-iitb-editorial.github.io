Bringing People Together and Helping Them, One Student at a Time

Department Academic Mentorship Program Coordinator 2025–2026: Maathangi S.

The Department Academic Mentorship Program (D-AMP) of the Mathematics Department is centred around supporting students through their academic and non-academic challenges through a structured program. With mentorship at the backbone of D-AMP, equal attention has been given to creating resources for students. Recognising that much of this guidance is often informal and dispersed, D-AMP aims to systematically compile and share it for the benefit of all students.

One of the key developments this academic year was the D-AMP website, which serves as a central platform for information dissemination. The website currently hosts the Course Reviews, which capture student experiences across courses. This resource provides insights into evaluation patterns, course structure, workload, and overall expectations, which help students make informed decisions when choosing and navigating courses. In addition, the team has also built a Resume and CV Repository, specific to the mathematics department. This serves as a reference for those preparing for higher studies or a career, providing direction and guidance.

Beyond the resources on paper, D-AMP also takes the initiative to conduct information dissemination sessions on various topics. For incoming sophomore students, Sophomore 101 was conducted in collaboration with UGAC to provide an introductory guide to navigating the department. Help sessions for various courses, such as Basic Algebra, PDE, and Functional Analysis, were conducted to provide academic assistance before exams. Apart from academic sessions, Resume building and Internship Info sessions were conducted for third-year and second-year students before their internship seasons, in collaboration with the PT Cell, by inviting seniors from various fields.

These resources and events are put together by a team of mentors working in Events, Web&Resource subgroups. Together, these resources and events contribute to building an effective pathway to ensure students make informed decisions within the departments. The aim of D-AMP remains to provide both mentorship in a personalised way and also provide static resources which can be passed on to generations of students yet to come.
